    Harm, in one unit

    Every shortfall is converted to household-hours without power: one household dark for one hour counts 1. The weights for a bed, a resident without water or signal, or a fire station are value judgements, shown on each card above. They are the only way to compare a dark hospital ward with a dark flat, and you should argue with them.
